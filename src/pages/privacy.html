Privacy policy

Effective Date: 11/07/2026

At Trainability LLC and Trainability Strength LLC (“we,” “our,” or “the Company”), we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a secure and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how we use it, and the steps we take to safeguard your data. By using our services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

Personal Information: When you register as a member, sign up for classes, or interact with our website, we may collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, emergency contact details, and payment information.

Photos: When you join, at our kiosk or online, we take or ask for a photo of you for your account, so our team can recognize you. It is kept with your account, and may be shown with your first name on screens inside our facilities, such as a studio schedule.

Health and Fitness Information: To tailor our services to your specific needs, we may collect health and fitness-related information such as medical history, fitness goals, dietary preferences, and injury information. This data is gathered only with your consent.

Usage Data: We collect information about your use of our website, the Trainability Studio app, and our services. This may include your IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited on our website. This helps us improve the functionality and user experience of our digital platforms.

Payment Information: For billing purposes, we may collect payment details such as credit card information or billing address. Payments are processed through secure, third-party payment processors, and we do not store sensitive payment information.

Text Message Information: If you agree to receive text messages from us, we collect your mobile phone number, your text message preferences, and your replies to our messages.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

To Provide Our Services: To process your membership, schedule training sessions, and provide access to fitness classes, studios, and other gym services.

To Communicate with You: To send membership updates, appointment reminders, class schedules, promotional offers, and other important information related to your membership or fitness journey.

To Personalize Your Experience: To customize our services, programs, and content based on your fitness goals, preferences, and health data.

To Improve Our Services: To analyze usage data, improve our website, mobile app, and overall customer experience, and develop new services to better meet your needs.

For Legal and Regulatory Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and industry standards, and to protect the rights and safety of our members, employees, and the public.

3. How We Protect Your Information

We take the security of your personal data seriously. We implement a variety of technical, physical, and administrative measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. These measures include:

  • Secure encryption for sensitive data transmissions (e.g., payment information).
  • Access controls to ensure that only authorized personnel can access your personal data.
  • Regular security audits to assess and address potential vulnerabilities.

However, please note that no data transmission or storage system can be completely secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties. Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in data and consent are handled as described under “Text Messaging (SMS)” below. However, we may share your data in the following circumstances:

With Service Providers: We may share your information with trusted third-party vendors who help us operate our business (e.g., payment processors, our membership-management system, cloud hosting, email, text-message and phone providers, AI providers, and IT support services). These service providers are required to handle your information securely and only for the purposes we define.

With Your Trainer: Many of our trainers are independent professionals. If you train with one, we share with them what they need to support your training, such as your appointments and sessions, your membership status and visits, and, if you cancel your membership, that you cancelled and the reason you gave.

AI and voice providers: Billy, our automated assistant, is powered by Anthropic. When you talk to Billy in the Trainability Studio app, on the phone, or on our website, what you say and the information he looks up to answer you are sent to Anthropic for that purpose. We also use Anthropic to summarize phone calls, to help our team write messages and read documents, and to frame and straighten member photos. Phone calls are carried by Twilio, and Billy’s voice is produced by ElevenLabs.

For Legal and Regulatory Compliance: We may disclose your information to comply with legal obligations, protect our rights, enforce our agreements, or respond to requests from government authorities.

In the Event of a Business Transfer: If the Company undergoes a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such changes in accordance with applicable law.

Text Messaging (SMS): If you agree to receive text messages from Trainability, we use your mobile phone number, preferences, and replies only to send you the account and service messages described in our Terms (such as sign-in codes, appointment reminders and updates, reservation, membership, and payment notices, and messages from your trainer or our team), and to act on your replies (for example, confirming or cancelling an appointment, or passing your reply to your trainer or our team). Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and consent will not be shared with or sold to third parties, affiliates, or lead generators for marketing or promotional purposes. We share them only with the service providers that help us run our services, such as the provider that delivers our messages and our membership-management system, and only for that purpose. You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP, or get help by replying HELP.

Phone calls: Calls to Billy’s number, {{billyPhone}}, are answered by Billy, our automated assistant. What callers say is turned into text while the call is in progress, so Billy can understand and answer. We don’t keep a recording or a transcript of the call: when it ends, a short summary of what it was about is kept with the call record so our team can follow up, together with any message the caller asks Billy to pass on. Callers can say “person” at any time to reach the team or leave a message.

Website chat: When you ask Billy a question in the chat on our website, your question and the conversation so far are sent to our Trainability Studio app, and to Anthropic, the AI provider that powers Billy, so he can answer. The chat does not use cookies or ask who you are. To keep out bots, a conversation is checked with Cloudflare Turnstile (see “Spam protection” below), and your IP address is used to limit how many questions can be sent. Please don’t type card numbers or other private details into it. Your browser keeps the conversation only until you close the tab or choose “Start over”.

Opt-Out of Marketing Communications: You can opt out of receiving promotional emails or other marketing communications from us by following the unsubscribe instructions in the communication or by contacting us.

Request Data Deletion: In certain circumstances, you may request that we delete your personal information. We will respond to your request in accordance with applicable laws, though some data may need to be retained for legal or operational purposes.

Data Portability: If applicable, you may request a copy of your personal data in a structured, commonly used format.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Analytics: To see how our website is used and improve it, we use Umami, a privacy-focused analytics service. Umami does not use cookies, does not collect your name, email address, or other information that identifies you, and does not follow you across other websites. It records the pages you visit, the website or link that brought you here, your browser, device type, and country, and anonymous actions such as tapping our phone number or a button. We do not see what you type into our forms through it, and we do not record your screen or your mouse movements.

Spam protection: Our online forms and the chat with Billy use Cloudflare Turnstile to check that a person, not a bot, is sending them. Cloudflare sees your browser and IP address for this check only; it sets no advertising cookies and doesn’t track you across sites.

Other services: Our website does not use advertising cookies. Some pages show content from other services, such as Google (fonts, maps, and reviews) and Trainability Studio (our forms and member sign-in), which may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If we become aware that we have, we will take steps to delete that information.
