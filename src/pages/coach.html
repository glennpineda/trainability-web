Programs, checked as you build

Design a client's program day by day. Coach IQ checks the whole program every time you change it.

  • Stop: swap it. An exercise the place has no equipment for, or one the client's limitations say to avoid.
  • Take care. Something to make easier for the client, or above their level.
  • Swap in one click. Exercises that could stand in, already fitting the place, the level and the limitations.

It's guidance: your word wins.

Billy, and your messages

Billy, the assistant from Studio, knows the clients on your list.

  • Ask about a client. Or about a program and what Coach IQ found.
  • Give him a photo or a PDF. A food log, a form a client filled in, a program, and ask about it.
  • Message your clients. Words, pictures, videos, their programs, measurements and test results.

Who this is for

Trainers at Trainability sign in with their Studio account. Training somewhere else? Make a Coach account and bring your clients.
