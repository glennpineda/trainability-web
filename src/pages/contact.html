You can cancel right here on this page, or in your member portal. Here’s how it goes here:

  1. Type the email on your membership and choose Email me a code or Text me a code.
  2. Enter the 6-digit code. That’s how we know it’s you, and nothing of yours shows before it.
  3. Your membership shows. Tap Cancel membership, and you’ll see the day it ends and any cancellation fee before you confirm.

Tell us at least 48 hours before your next payment and that payment isn’t charged. With less notice it’s still charged, and your membership ends when the period it covers is over. Changed your mind? Keep my membership takes it back.

In your member portal it’s the Membership tab, then Cancel membership.

You can pause right here on this page, or in your member portal. Here’s how it goes here:

  1. Type the email on your membership and choose Email me a code or Text me a code.
  2. Enter the 6-digit code. That’s how we know it’s you, and nothing of yours shows before it.
  3. Your membership shows. Tap Pause, then pick the day it starts and the day you’re back. You’ll see how many of this year’s pauses you’ve used.

Back early? End the pause starts your membership again, and Call off the pause cancels one that hasn’t started. A membership can’t be paused while a payment is owed.

In your member portal it’s the Membership tab, then Pause.
