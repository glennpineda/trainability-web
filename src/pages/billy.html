What he does for you

  • Answers your questions. Memberships, day passes, prices, hours, our two locations and our services, any time of day.
  • Knows your schedule. In your member portal, ask "when is my next session?" or "how many sessions do I have left?"
  • Shows you the way. He tells you exactly where to tap for what you want to do, on the screen you're on.
  • Picks up the phone. He tells you when you're next in, where each place is and when it's open, cancels a session when you ask (up to 24 hours before), takes a message, and puts you through to a person when someone's here.
  • Reads aloud. In the app, he can say his answers out loud, so you can follow along while you tap.

What he does for our trainers

  • Plans the day. Their appointments, birthdays and milestones to celebrate, and who's running low on sessions.
  • Knows the building. Who's in the rooms right now, and where there's a free studio tomorrow morning.
  • Helps design your training. In Coach, he suggests exercises that fit the equipment, your level and any limitations, and reads a food log or a form your trainer gives him.
  • Leaves the decisions to people. Your trainer always has the final word on your program.

You can see what he's doing

Billy's face shows what he's up to: listening while you type, thinking while he works it out, and talking when he answers.

Helpful, and careful

  • He's an assistant, not a person. Billy is a computer program, and he says so. Ask for a person on the phone at any time.
  • He only sees what's yours. In your member portal he sees your own appointments, sessions and payments, and nobody else's.
  • You stay in control. In the app he never books, buys or changes anything for you: he shows you the button, and you press it.
  • He's not a doctor. About pain, an injury or anything medical, he'll tell you to see a professional.
  • He can get things wrong. Like any assistant, he can make mistakes, so check anything important with our team.
  • Keep private details private. Don't type card numbers or anything sensitive into a chat with him. Read more in our privacy policy.
