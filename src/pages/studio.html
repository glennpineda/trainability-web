Reserve a studio in three taps

A member picks today or tomorrow, a studio and a time, and it is held for them.

  • You see what is free. Times already taken have a line through them.
  • You move it or cancel it. One reservation a day, and it is yours to change.
  • Your trainer books your sessions. A session with a trainer appears in your member portal as soon as it is booked.

Show up and see it count

Every check-in at the front door is a visit, and your member portal keeps the count.

  • Visits and weeks in a row. How many times you have come in, and how many weeks running.
  • Milestones are marked. Your 10th, 25th, 50th and 100th visit, and 4, 8, 12, 26 and 52 weeks in a row.
  • Sessions left, and why. How many sessions you have with your trainer, and how each one was counted.

Your team’s hours, from the time clock straight to payroll.

Clock in and out with a PIN

Hourly staff tap their picture at the time clock and type a PIN. They see their shift straight away, split into Regular and Overtime under California’s rules, with their week and pay period so far.

Your team’s hours go straight to payroll

At the end of the pay period, check the hours, settle any shift that needs a look and mark them reviewed. One click sends them to QuickBooks payroll, overtime already worked out. Seamless integration with QuickBooks to pay your team. Nobody types hours in twice.

Built into Studio

Billy, at your side

Billy is the assistant inside Studio. He knows the screen you're on and who you are, so you can ask in your own words.

  • In the Billy panel. Tap his face in Studio and ask: "when is my next session?", "how many sessions do I have left?" He looks it up and shows you where to tap, and can read his answers aloud.
  • On the phone. Call {{billyPhone}} and Billy answers. From the mobile number on your account he knows it's you: when you’re next in, canceling a session up to 24 hours before, or a message for the team.
  • For trainers and the desk. He plans the day: appointments, birthdays and milestones, who's running low on sessions, who's in the rooms and where there's a free studio.
  • Around the gym. Each night he writes what's in the studio's new playlist, for the screens and for you to follow.
