Keep every appointment in one place

Your member portal lists what is booked for the next 12 weeks, day by day, with who it is with and where.

Confirm with a Y

A reminder comes 48 hours before. Reply Y and the appointment shows Confirmed.

Put it in your calendar

Add one appointment, or all of them. A session that moves changes in your calendar too.

Cancel in time, yourself

Up to 24 hours before, cancel in your member portal and your trainer is told. After that, contact your trainer.

Reserve a studio in three taps

A member picks today or tomorrow, a studio and a time, and it is held for them.

  • You see what is free. Times already taken have a line through them.
  • You move it or cancel it. One reservation a day, and it is yours to change.
  • Your trainer books your sessions. A session with a trainer appears in your member portal as soon as it is booked.

Show up and see it count

Every check-in at the front door is a visit, and your member portal keeps the count.

  • Visits and weeks in a row. How many times you have come in, and how many weeks running.
  • Milestones are marked. Your 10th, 25th, 50th and 100th visit, and 4, 8, 12, 26 and 52 weeks in a row.
  • Sessions left, and why. How many sessions you have with your trainer, and how each one was counted.
