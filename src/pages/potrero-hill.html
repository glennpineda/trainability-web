Why choose between personal training and an open gym?

Potrero Hill brings together the personal, bespoke feel of our Mission studios and the flexibility of an open gym. You get the same personal attention that made our first location a hit, plus a place to train on your own whenever you like.

We don't want to be a big-box gym. We find them sterile, impersonal and, frankly, not fun. Here, we build an inclusive, communal place that makes you feel part of our family, because you are. We want to know you, and we want you to know us.

The gym is in a renovated, formerly industrial space designed to make working out fun. Relax in the generous seating area with reading material, a fireplace and even a massage chair, all in the simple, modern European design our Nordic founder and owner curated.
