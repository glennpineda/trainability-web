Pausing and cancelling

Pause

You can pause your membership for any reason while your account is in good standing: up to two months at a time, twice a year, with at least two months between pauses. Longer pauses are possible for valid medical reasons.

Cancel

You can cancel at any time. Your membership stays active until the end of the current billing cycle, so cancel before the next one starts. Payments already processed aren't refunded.

Cancelling a recurring membership less than two months after signing up has a $50 cancellation fee. Promotional, single-month and other prepaid memberships have no cancellation fee.

To pause, send us a pause request. To cancel, send us a cancellation request.
