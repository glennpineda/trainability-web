Pausing and cancelling

Pause

You can pause your membership while your account is in good standing, from 14 days up to 2 months at a time, twice in each membership year (counted from your start date). After a pause, the next one can start once as many days have passed as the last pause lasted. A membership can't be paused while a payment is owed, before it starts, or after you've asked to cancel. Longer pauses are possible for valid medical reasons.

Cancel

You can cancel at any time, in your member portal or by sending us a cancellation request. Your membership stays active until the end of what you've paid for, and nothing more is charged after that. Please give us at least 48 hours' notice before your next payment; with less, that payment is still charged and your membership ends at the close of the period it covers.

Changed your mind? Cancel within five business days of joining and your membership ends that day, with no cancellation fee, and everything you paid is refunded.

Cancelling a recurring membership after only one billing cycle has a $50 cancellation fee. There's no fee if you move more than 25 miles away, become disabled, or we close or reduce the facility, and none on promotional, single-month or other prepaid memberships.

To pause, send us a pause request. To cancel, send us a cancellation request.
